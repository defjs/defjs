# Concurrency limits & pooled calls

Use `concurrencyLimitHttpInterceptor` on the `Client` as a client-wide bulkhead (FIFO queueing with optional `maxQueue` overflow protection), and use `@defjs/core/concurrent` (`concurrentMap`, `concurrentMapSettled`, `concurrentAll`, `concurrentAllSettled`) for fail-fast or settled parallel calls bound directly to that `Client`.
